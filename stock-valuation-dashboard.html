Overview

This dashboard delivers a full-spectrum equity valuation workflow inside a single Excel workbook — from raw financial statement data through to a probability-weighted intrinsic value consensus and a BUY / SELL signal.

Built to support retail investment research on US equities, it replaces ad-hoc spreadsheets with a repeatable, institutionally structured process. Any ticker available on StockAnalysis.com can be loaded through the Power Query pipeline without rebuilding the model — reducing a full valuation from hours to minutes.

Annual by default. Convertible to quarterly or trailing-twelve-months (TTM) view without structural changes to the data model. Dashboard language reflects the primary investment market (English / USD).

Data & Methodology

Financial data is sourced entirely from StockAnalysis.com Pro, covering 10 years of annual statements across income, balance sheet, cash flow, and ratios. Price and market data is pulled from Yahoo Finance, and company profile metadata from the Stock Data add-in for Excel. All sources are integrated through a unified Power Query pipeline.

Dashboard Sheets

The workbook is organized into six analytical sheets, each serving a distinct role in the valuation process — from screening fundamentals to synthesizing a final investment signal.

Sheet 01
Stock Screener
One-page snapshot: profitability, per-share metrics, liquidity, solvency, FCF, dividend data, growth rates, and market valuation. Includes price chart and earnings/cash flow history over 10 years.
Sheet 02
Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)
10-year FCF projection with configurable growth rate, WACC, and terminal growth rate. Full equity bridge, share-count adjustment, and a two-dimensional sensitivity matrix (WACC × terminal growth rate).
Sheet 03
Graham's Valuation
Both Graham formulas side-by-side: the 1962 Original (no rate adjustment) and the 1974 Revised (AAA bond yield–adjusted). Supports historical EPS or forward EPS as inputs.
Sheet 04
Comparable Company Multiples
Peer group valuation on P/E and EV/EBIT. Configurable peer set with automatic implied price calculation relative to subject company fundamentals.
Sheet 05
Historical Multiples
10-year trailing history of P/E, P/B, P/S, EV/EBIT, and FCF Yield. Benchmarks TTM multiples against their 10-year average and median to flag compression or expansion vs. historical norms.
Sheet 06
Intrinsic Value Summary
Consolidates all four methods into a single view. Displays average and median intrinsic values, applies a configurable margin of safety, and outputs a consensus BUY / SELL signal with upside / downside percentage.

Business Value

The dashboard addresses the core challenge of methodological inconsistency in retail investment research: each valuation model (DCF, Graham's, multiples) can produce different results in isolation. By structuring all four methods inside a single normalized data model and forcing an explicit consensus output, it imposes discipline on the process.

For a portfolio manager, analyst, or serious individual investor, this workbook replaces scattered models with a single, audit-ready file that clearly separates manual inputs (blue), formula outputs (black), and cross-sheet links (green) — the same color convention used in institutional DCF models.

  • Investment screening — Filter any US equity by fundamental quality before committing to a full valuation
  • Valuation synthesis — Aggregate four independent methods to reduce anchoring to any single estimate
  • Repeatable process — New ticker loaded via Power Query refresh; no structural rebuild required
  • Client reporting — Clean, print-ready layout suitable for investment committee presentations or client deliverables

Designed for annual analysis of US-listed equities. A parallel workflow for BVC (Colombian exchange) equities using Supersociedades data is covered under a separate project.
